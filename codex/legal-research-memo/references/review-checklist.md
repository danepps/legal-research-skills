# Pre-Delivery Review Checklist

Use this as a final pass. Mark each item complete, not applicable, or needing follow-up.

## Assignment and scope

- [ ] Question Presented is answerable and matches the user’s request.
- [ ] Jurisdiction, court, date range, governing law, and research cutoff are stated.
- [ ] Facts, assumptions, disputed facts, and exclusions are separated.
- [ ] The source universe and any claim of completeness are accurately described.

## Authority

- [ ] Every material proposition has a source.
- [ ] The source actually supports the proposition at the level of generality used.
- [ ] Each material claim is identifiable as a holding/rule, synthesis, inference, analogy, or proposal.
- [ ] Each material case/source has an accurate analytical role, classification status, and precedential effect; assumed or arguendo baselines, pluralities, and equally divided dispositions are not presented as majority holdings.
- [ ] Each material source identifies its coverage cells and distinct contribution rather than merely repeating a proposition already in the ledger.
- [ ] Every named scholar, work, case, or source-specific characterization in the prose has a nearby citation or footnote and a matching ledger entry.
- [ ] Binding and persuasive authorities are labeled and ordered correctly.
- [ ] Current text and later treatment were checked where currentness matters.
- [ ] Adverse authority, contrary terminology, and meaningful splits were searched.
- [ ] Historical sources are not presented as current law without explanation.

## Citation and quotation

- [ ] Case names, court, year, reporter/docket, and statutory citations are accurate.
- [ ] Pinpoints lead to the relied-on language or reasoning.
- [ ] Every quotation was checked against the underlying source.
- [ ] Omissions, brackets, emphasis, and attribution are accurate.
- [ ] Holdings, dicta, separate opinions, party arguments, and inference are distinguished.
- [ ] Short cites are unambiguous and used only after a full citation.
- [ ] Verification labels are accurate, and any global verification statement matches the weakest material source.
- [ ] Case-citation existence was checked deterministically when the service was available, without overstating what the check proves.

## Analysis

- [ ] The memo leads with a bottom line.
- [ ] Each subquestion has a rule, synthesis, application, counterargument, and conclusion where needed.
- [ ] For schema-v6 standard/deep work, every included dispositive/material claim has a current unique draft anchor, memo heading, analytical role, and linked supporting/adverse source IDs.
- [ ] Distinct doctrinal stages, elements, and alternative routes are separated before synthesis.
- [ ] Contextual factors are not presented as universal elements unless the governing authority makes them so.
- [ ] The memo explains why authorities matter instead of reciting facts.
- [ ] The strongest argument against the conclusion is stated fairly.
- [ ] Uncertainty is calibrated and tied to a concrete source, fact, or doctrinal gap.
- [ ] No frequency, priority, or universal claim (“no court,” “settled,” “always,” “first,” “earliest,” “overwhelmingly,” “most”) is left unqualified without a documented coverage protocol.
- [ ] The final deliverable's schema-v4-and-newer quantifier scan is current, and every trigger occurrence maps to a protocol-backed claim or an explicit occurrence-specific waiver.
- [ ] A schema-v5-and-newer standard/deep scholarly or hybrid run passed `direct_source_audit` with `event:direct_source_search` evidence and a verified `direct_answer` source when one exists.
- [ ] Cross-doctrinal analogies identify what is transferred, why, and what limits the analogy.
- [ ] Deep or high-stakes work includes a bounded refutation pass on the three to five load-bearing authorities.

## Scholarly mode, when applicable

- [ ] The project states a research problem and provisional or final thesis, not merely a topic.
- [ ] The relevant literature is synthesized by debate, method, or problem rather than listed author by author.
- [ ] A direct-answer core is separated from component, adjacent, and background sources; the closest direct work was citation-chased in both directions and likely direct candidates were promoted to full text.
- [ ] Any statement that no directly responsive work was located records the bounded search, cutoff, source universe or databases, and material access limits rather than inferring absence from adjacent abstracts.
- [ ] The source base, selection criteria, provenance, and archival or evidentiary gaps are described.
- [ ] Historical period boundaries and transition evidence are explicit, and out-of-period sources are not silently counted as in-period proof.
- [ ] Historical work separates its conceptions taxonomy from its epoch-and-transition chronology when multiple models coexist.
- [ ] Every material planned coverage cell has a mapped material claim and directly verified source, or the gap is disclosed and completion is limited.
- [ ] Selection effects, assumed baselines, archival negative space, and material historical harm or exclusion were considered without turning silence into proof.
- [ ] Terms are defined in historical context and not silently projected backward.
- [ ] Rival interpretations and inconvenient evidence are addressed.
- [ ] The claimed contribution is no broader than the evidence supports.

## Specialized methods, when applicable

- [ ] Statutory/regulatory work uses the text and version in force, maps legal-force hierarchy, and distinguishes proposed, effective, stayed, and superseded material.
- [ ] Comparative/foreign/international work states the jurisdiction-specific hierarchy, authoritative language and translation limits, and the basis for jurisdiction selection and functional comparison.
- [ ] Empirical work states the population, sample, unit, variable definitions, denominator, provenance, uncertainty, design limits, and claim type.
- [ ] Record-intensive work distinguishes allegations, evidence, findings, concessions, and argument and ties material facts to the governing elements and procedural posture.

## Deliverable

- [ ] Headings, topic sentences, citations, and cross-references are internally consistent.
- [ ] Selected bibliographies and tables of authorities use complete citations and do not omit a first page, year, author, title, or stable location that was available in the ledger.
- [ ] The memo is readable in the requested format.
- [ ] If a DOCX was created, its OOXML package, XML parts, hashes, and extracted text passed structural verification; the deliverable states that pagination and visual layout were not rendered.
- [ ] Confidential or privileged material was handled within the authorized environment.
- [ ] A short research-status or limitations note is included when useful.
- [ ] Deep/high-stakes work, and standard work with mixed verification, discloses per-authority verification status proportionately.
- [ ] The run manifest records the exact deliverable path and available provenance; timestamps were produced only by the run scripts.
- [ ] Currentness conclusions identify the service, query or citator action, date, result, and limitations for controlling/load-bearing authority.
- [ ] CourtListener treatment probes, when used, were treated as leads only; material hits were opened, and neither no hits nor citation recency was converted into a validity or dormancy conclusion.
